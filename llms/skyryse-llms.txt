# Skyryse

> Skyryse is an El Segundo, California aviation technology company founded in 2016 by Mark Groden and Mitch Adler. It builds SkyOS, a universal operating system for flight — a retrofittable hardware and software stack that replaces mechanical flight controls with a triply redundant, software-driven system, letting a pilot fly an airplane or helicopter with a single control stick and touchscreens. Skyryse also produces Skyryse One, a SkyOS-powered production helicopter, and Skylar, a universal AI flight assistant. In February 2026 the company raised over $300 million in Series C funding at a $1.15 billion post-money valuation.

## Products

- [SkyOS](https://www.skyryse.com/skyos): Universal operating system for flight — simplified controls, inherent stability, automated maneuvers, emergency autorotation, automated zero-visibility landing, envelope protection, optionally piloted capability.
- [Skyryse One](https://www.skyryse.com/skyryse-one): SkyOS-powered production helicopter with automated engine start, set-down, autorotation and stable hover.
- [Civil Aircraft](https://www.skyryse.com/aircraft): SkyOS retrofit program for civil airplanes and helicopters.
- [Defense Aircraft](https://www.skyryse.com/defense): SkyOS for military platforms, including the first flight of a Black Hawk under SkyOS.
